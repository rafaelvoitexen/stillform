# STILLFORM — Experience narrative

**Phase 02 · 25 October 2026 · Proposed, awaiting human approval**

This document defines exactly four chapters. Each chapter has an A and B treatment so the unselected direction remains a complete experience. These are alternatives, not eight sections or a recommendation to combine them. [Direction systems and recommendation](ART_DIRECTION_PROPOSAL.md) · [evidence board](VISUAL_REFERENCE_BOARD.md).

**A — Occupied Void:** encounter an opening → learn to read the interval → compare four spatial observations → return with a changed understanding.

**B — Chromatic Memory:** encounter a place and its trace → understand why a detail stays with us → compare four view/detail pairs → retain one final association.

All sample copy is original draft editorial language. No building names, dates, architects or photographers are invented. Real asset metadata is a later verification task. “Notes” in the navigation points to credits within the Outro; it does not create a fifth chapter. Each treatment below has exactly one signature interaction per chapter. Ordinary anchors, focus feedback and readable controls are functional behavior rather than additional spectacles.

## 01 — HERO

**Narrative purpose:** announce a point of view before asking the visitor to explore. STILLFORM is an editorial archive, not an architectural practice selling services.

### A — Occupied Void

- **Primary composition:** a compact left-hand statement and a dominant 5:4 architectural photograph on the right, separated by one empty grid interval. The photograph's left edge establishes the alignment that later chapters inherit. Masthead and direct chapter links remain small.
- **Main content:** “Space, / held / still.” Supporting line: “An editorial archive of architecture, light and the spaces between.” One photograph caption and its real credit.
- **ONE signature interaction — opening the interval:** on arrival, a single planar reveal makes the photograph fully visible from the recurring edge. The image remains spatially stable; the transition makes the opening inside the architecture noticeable.
- **Relationship to the next chapter:** the photograph's edge becomes the Manifesto's reading edge. A detail of the same opening reappears there, making the transition one of attention rather than subject.
- **Mobile authorship:** two-line title above a dominant 4:5 crop; caption inset replaces the desktop empty column. The image starts within the initial screen; the entire Hero need not fit within it.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **High.** Visual weight and a precise absence create the first impression before motion is considered.

### B — Chromatic Memory

- **Primary composition:** a large, conversational serif statement above a contextual photograph; a smaller detail to the right shares its lower edge. The two photographs belong to the same space, with one linking observation.
- **Main content:** “Some spaces / stay with us.” Supporting line: “Four observations on light, color and material.” A joint caption describes what the detail reveals.
- **ONE signature interaction — forming a pair:** the smaller detail joins the already legible context view as one quiet association on arrival. It does not cycle automatically or animate the building's lighting.
- **Relationship to the next chapter:** the detail becomes the Manifesto's subject. The Hero supplies the place; the following text explains the act of noticing.
- **Mobile authorship:** statement and contextual view first, then a sufficiently large detail with its sentence. Preserve discovery through reading order, rather than squeezing the pair into thumbnails.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **High, with a slower emotional arrival than A.** A specific visual rhyme gives the visitor a reason to look twice.

## 02 — MANIFESTO

**Narrative purpose:** articulate the archive's editorial method in a short, readable passage. This chapter earns the visitor's attention for the Archive; it is not an oversized slogan repeated over several screens.

### A — Occupied Void

- **Primary composition:** a small crop of the Hero's opening on the left; an opening statement and compact serif passage begin on the established right-side alignment. The scale changes from monument to observation, while the background stays mineral.
- **Main content:** heading “The space between is part of the form.” Draft passage: “A wall is understood by the light beside it. An opening makes a mass legible. STILLFORM collects these relationships: how geometry directs a gaze, how shadow gives depth, and how a material holds the passing light.” Keep the final passage around 60–90 words; no service claims.
- **ONE signature interaction — holding a point of reference:** on roomy desktop screens, the small image holds its position while the short observation passes beside it. The reading text stays fully legible. The purpose is to compare a stable spatial fact with an evolving interpretation.
- **Continuity from/to adjacent chapters:** reuse the Hero crop and keep its major edge as the prose alignment. The final sentence, “The archive begins with four ways of looking,” sits immediately before the first record. The Archive inherits the same figure/caption relationship.
- **Mobile authorship:** place the detail above the prose in natural flow. The deliberate hold becomes spatial proximity; no sticky stage or scroll wait.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **Medium–High.** The chapter changes the meaning of the first image without introducing another visual vocabulary.

### B — Chromatic Memory

- **Primary composition:** a pale-chalk reading field within the lavender page. A short serif essay sits beside one close detail of reflected light or surface. The detail's baseline and caption relate to the Hero pair; there is no permanent vertical spine.
- **Main content:** heading “We remember the light before the plan.” Draft passage: “A room returns as a color, a cool surface, a patch of light on a stair. STILLFORM looks at architecture through these traces. Each view is paired with something easily missed, inviting the eye to move between a place and the detail that stays with us.” Target 60–90 words after editing.
- **ONE signature interaction — noticing the trace:** the close detail becomes visible as its observation enters the reading area, then stays present. This is a single reveal of a related piece of evidence, not a sequence of fading paragraphs.
- **Continuity from/to adjacent chapters:** the Hero's trace is now examined; the last line, “Look at the whole, then look again,” introduces the Archive's view/detail relationship. The pair returns at a more practical scale.
- **Mobile authorship:** reading passage first, detail and caption immediately after. Short lines and comfortable type replace the desktop side-by-side relationship.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **Medium–High.** The visual tenderness gains an explicit editorial reason.

## 03 — ARCHIVE

**Narrative purpose:** prove the thesis through a small set of specific observations. This is the densest and most useful chapter, not a decorative gallery separating two large slogans.

Shared content scope: **four records** under the draft themes Threshold, Interval, Weight and Reflection. Each includes a real architectural photograph, verified attribution, a short title and a 40–70-word observation. Theme ordering remains editable with the actual photographs. No search, filters, account, endless feed, case-study routes or backend are required.

### A — Occupied Void

- **Primary composition:** varied figure scales within the same grid. One large landscape starts the chapter; a narrower portrait and a medium landscape create a more compact middle; the last record restores a clear opening. Captions attach closely to figures. There is no identical card background or automatic checkerboard.
- **Main content:** each record describes one visible relationship, such as a threshold compressing a view or a shadow making a wall's thickness legible. Do not claim structural facts that cannot be established from verified source material.
- **ONE signature interaction — reading into the record:** “Read observation” expands a concise note directly under the selected figure. The figure remains the stable reference; the expansion preserves reading position and has a clear close state. No modal, zoom surface or hover-only reveal is added.
- **Continuity from/to adjacent chapters:** the first figure/caption takes over the Manifesto's alignment. The final record returns to the opening motif, preparing the Outro's reused detail. Record spacing grows or contracts with editorial importance, not with arbitrary scroll lengths.
- **Mobile authorship:** all four records form one ordered sequence with alternating useful ratios, not alternating alignments. Title and disclosure belong directly below each image. Reading the last line never requires precise scroll choreography.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **High through editing and composition; Medium from the interaction itself.** Senior craft appears in how comfortably the evidence can be explored.

### B — Chromatic Memory

- **Primary composition:** four compact photographic essays, each with a stable main frame, a visible relation to a detail, and close-set title/observation. Vary relative image emphasis within the eight-column system without repeating the Hero spread four times.
- **Main content:** the observation connects context to a particular trace: a reflected hue, a material junction, a shadow boundary or the thickness of a stair. Names and credits remain visible independently of the selected image.
- **ONE signature interaction — View / Detail:** an explicit two-state control exchanges the contextual photograph and its related detail within a stable frame. The accompanying caption identifies the selected view. It is an intentional request to look again, not an automatic carousel.
- **Continuity from/to adjacent chapters:** the Manifesto's instruction becomes a useful control. The last pair is quiet enough to let the Outro return to the initial visual association without a new color spectacle.
- **Mobile authorship:** one generous main frame with the two labeled controls and caption beneath it. The relation remains understandable without side-by-side comparison, hover or swiping. A static paired arrangement remains the fallback.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **High if the pairs reveal something meaningful.** A weak crop cannot be rescued by a polished crossfade.

## 04 — OUTRO

**Narrative purpose:** leave a final thought, expose authorship and sources, and let the visitor return deliberately. This is a colophon and a closing image, not a contact-sales funnel.

### A — Occupied Void

- **Primary composition:** a compact moss field with chalk text; a crop of the Hero's opening sits against the same recurring alignment. The crop now emphasizes the empty space rather than the surrounding mass. Keep this chapter shorter than the Hero and place credits where they can be read.
- **Main content:** “The opening remains.” Follow with: “STILLFORM is an independent editorial study of architecture and perception.” Add actual design/development credit, image credits/source links and the explicit note “Fictional editorial project.” A portfolio link may be added when its real destination is supplied.
- **ONE signature interaction — returning to the opening:** “Return to the beginning” uses the ordinary document anchor. It returns to the already settled Hero, without replaying an intro or forcing another reveal. The changed reading of the same photograph is the payoff.
- **Continuity:** the final crop and alignment close the loop established by the Hero. Moss is a compact resting surface, not a new full-site dark theme.
- **Mobile authorship:** short closing statement, image crop, credits and return link in natural order. No fixed footer conceals the final record.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **Medium–High.** Repetition with a changed emphasis gives the experience an authored ending.

### B — Chromatic Memory

- **Primary composition:** return to the pale-chalk reading surface. The first detail appears once more as a modest image, with the final sentence beside it. No additional unrelated photograph competes with that remembered trace.
- **Main content:** “A detail becomes a place.” Follow with the same factual colophon and project disclosure as A. The photo credit belongs beside the remembered detail as well as in the full credits.
- **ONE signature interaction — revisiting the pair:** “Return to the beginning” takes the visitor to the settled Hero pair through the ordinary document anchor. There is no slideshow, regenerated pair or looping animation.
- **Continuity:** the Hero's second image becomes the last image. The same visual association is encountered with more understanding after four records.
- **Mobile authorship:** detail, sentence, credit and return link form a small closing composition. It should feel conclusive without requiring a full-screen finale.
- **Expected artistic impact:** **Medium–High.** The ending reinforces intimacy and retention rather than adding a final technical flourish.

**Approval boundary:** these four chapters define intent and continuity only. Section heights, keyframes, trigger positions, durations and detailed implementation remain open for the subsequent approved phase.
